Conducting customer research interviews and distributing research surveys for
**Verazoi**, a student startup, through the PLASMA Accelerator.

To keep research moving without manual overhead, I built a workflow that automates
interview scheduling by connecting Qualtrics and Calendly — so recruiting and booking
participants runs itself while the team focuses on the conversations.
