Designed and built the homepage for **COFFEE** — the Club of Female/Femme
Electrical Engineers at UC Davis — using Figma and React, featuring a hero section
and an image carousel.

A from-scratch build spanning design and front-end: wireframes and visual design in
Figma, then implemented as a live React site. Visit it at
[coffeeatucd.com](https://coffeeatucd.com).
