Led user research analyzing why 30+ local businesses closed in Davis, then designed
data-driven solutions to boost foot traffic and student engagement downtown.

The work paired survey design and data analysis with a design response: understanding
the stakeholders (students, residents, and local business owners), surfacing the
conflicts between them, and translating those findings into concrete recommendations
for a more resilient downtown.
