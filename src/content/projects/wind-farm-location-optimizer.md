As an **AI Fellow at AI4ALL**, I prototyped and developed a machine learning
algorithm to optimize wind farm siting across the United States, combining
geospatial and environmental data for sustainable energy planning.

## What it does

The model takes real-world locations and predicts their suitability for wind
energy, learning from environmental and geospatial features to rank candidate
sites — turning a slow, manual siting analysis into a data-driven recommendation.

## What I did

- Built and trained the ML pipeline in Python (feature engineering over
  geospatial + environmental datasets).
- Evaluated predictions against real U.S. locations to validate siting quality.
- Worked in a 5-person team over ~8 months through the AI4ALL fellowship.
