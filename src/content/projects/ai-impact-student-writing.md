As a Research Assistant for the **PAIRR (Peer & AI Review + Reflection)** project
at UC Davis, I contribute to a study measuring how AI tools impact student writing
ability, course performance, and attitudes toward AI across community colleges and
universities.

## Problem

AI writing tools are being adopted rapidly in higher education, but there's limited
empirical data on how different AI interventions affect writing outcomes. Educators
need evidence on which tools help vs. hinder learning, and how student perceptions
of AI shift after structured exposure in English courses.

## What I did

- Processed and cleaned survey data from **500+ college students** across multiple
  institutions to measure AI attitude shifts before and after course completion.
- Transcribed and analyzed qualitative interviews to assess how AI tools affected
  writing ability and course performance.
- Organized final essays and student reflections comparing peer review,
  AI-generated feedback, and hybrid approaches.

## Approach

A pre/post survey design paired with qualitative interview analysis: survey design
and distribution, cross-institution data cleaning, qualitative coding of interview
themes, and comparative analysis of attitude shifts across intervention types.

The work contributes to peer-reviewed research on AI in education, and the findings
will help guide institutional decisions on AI-tool adoption in writing curricula.
